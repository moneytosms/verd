Codeforces, one key at a time.

verd is a terminal app for Codeforces and CSES. Read the problem, solve it in Neovim, test, stress and submit without opening a browser.

Install verd

The whole loop in 88 seconds

Sound on. Every terminal frame in the film is verd's own output, driven by real key presses on a real problem.

Local verdict: wrong answer

AC, WA, TLE, RE, MLE and CE are decided on your machine, with time and memory per test, before you spend a real submission. A failure says why in plain words and points at the source line when it can.

Get Accepted.

    
One binary in ~/.local/bin, checksum verified. Linux and macOS, amd64 and arm64; on Windows, inside WSL. You also need nvim and a compiler for your languages.
